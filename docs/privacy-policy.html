Privacy Policy

clipSlim by AppyAccidents — Last updated March 5, 2026

The short version: clipSlim does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything happens locally on your Mac.

Data Collection

clipSlim collects no data whatsoever. Specifically:

  • No personal information
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No crash reports
  • No cookies or tracking identifiers
  • No advertising data

Network Access

clipSlim makes zero network requests. The app does not connect to the internet for any reason. All image processing is performed entirely on your device using Apple's built-in ImageIO framework.

Clipboard Access

clipSlim monitors your macOS clipboard to detect copied images and optimize them. Clipboard contents are processed locally in memory and are never stored externally or transmitted. You can pause or disable clipboard monitoring at any time from the menubar.

File Access

When the Folder Watcher feature is enabled, clipSlim reads images from folders you designate and writes optimized versions to an output folder on your Mac. These files remain entirely on your local disk.

Third-Party Services

clipSlim uses no third-party SDKs, services, or frameworks beyond Apple's native macOS APIs. There are no third-party analytics, advertising, or data processing services.

Children's Privacy

clipSlim does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date. Since clipSlim collects no data, material changes are unlikely.

Contact
