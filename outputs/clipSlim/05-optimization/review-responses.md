## v1.1-Specific Response Templates

### AVIF Quality Concern
```
AVIF compression quality can vary depending on the source image and quality setting. Try increasing the quality slider in Settings > Custom preset. If you're still seeing artifacts, email us at support@appyaccidents.com with a sample image and we'll investigate.
```

### Shortcuts Not Working
```
Make sure you're running macOS 14.0 or later and that ClipSlim has been opened at least once after the v1.1 update. The OptimizeImageIntent should appear in Shortcuts.app automatically. If it doesn't, try restarting Shortcuts.app. If the issue persists, email support@appyaccidents.com.
```

### Finder Quick Action Not Appearing
```
The Finder Quick Action registers when ClipSlim is first launched after the v1.1 update. Try quitting and reopening ClipSlim, then right-click an image file in Finder. If it still doesn't appear, email support@appyaccidents.com with your macOS version.
```

### Metadata Control Question
```
In Settings, you can choose which metadata to keep (like copyright) and which to strip (like GPS coordinates). Look for the Metadata section in your optimization preset settings.
```

## Response Guidelines

1. **Always respond within 24 hours** -- Apple shows response time to potential users
2. **Never be defensive** -- acknowledge the feedback, offer a path forward
3. **Route to email for complex issues** -- keep review responses concise
4. **Thank every reviewer** -- even negative ones (they took time to write)
5. **Mention specific features** when relevant -- it helps with discoverability
6. **Never ask for a rating change** -- against Apple guidelines
7. **Keep responses under 3 sentences** for reviews, longer is fine for Reddit/social
