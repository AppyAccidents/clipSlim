## Handoff: What to Monitor

The most important metrics to watch, in order:
1. **Rating count** -- 0 ratings is the #1 conversion killer right now
2. **Conversion rate** -- tells you if the metadata and screenshots are working
3. **Keyword impressions** -- tells you if the keyword strategy is generating visibility
4. **Download volume** -- the ultimate success metric
5. **Crash reports** -- any v1.1-specific crashes need immediate attention
