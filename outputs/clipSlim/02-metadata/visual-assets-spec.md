## Screenshot Specifications

### Mac App Store Requirements
- **Resolution:** 2560 x 1600 pixels (Retina 16" MacBook Pro) OR 2880 x 1800 pixels
- **Format:** PNG or JPEG
- **Count:** Up to 10 screenshots
- **First screenshot is critical** -- visible in search results and browse

### Recommended Screenshot Set (v1.1)

| # | Content | Caption Overlay | v1.1 Feature? |
|---|---------|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | Menubar panel with compression result | "Compress images the moment you copy them" | No (core) |
| 2 | AVIF format selection / output result | "Native AVIF encoding -- next-gen format" | **Yes** |
| 3 | Before/after comparison slider | "See the difference before you commit" | **Yes** |
| 4 | Shortcuts.app with OptimizeImageIntent | "Automate with Apple Shortcuts" | **Yes** |
| 5 | Finder right-click context menu | "Right-click to optimize in Finder" | **Yes** |
| 6 | Drop Zone with multiple files | "Drag and drop up to 100 images" | No (core) |
| 7 | Selective metadata toggles | "Keep copyright. Strip GPS. You choose." | **Yes** |
| 8 | App-to-preset mapping settings | "Different apps, different presets" | **Yes** |
| 9 | Folder watcher configuration | "Watch folders for automatic optimization" | No (core) |
| 10 | Privacy statement screen | "100% local. Zero uploads. Your clipboard is yours." | No (trust) |

### Design Guidelines
- Background: Dark (#1A1A2E or similar), neon terminal aesthetic
- Caption font: SF Pro Display Bold, white, top 20% of screenshot
- Accent colors: Neon cyan (#00F5FF) and orange (#FF8A00)
- App UI: Actual screenshots, centered, not mockups
- Captions must be readable at search-result thumbnail size
